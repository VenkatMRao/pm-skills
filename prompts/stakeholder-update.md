# Stakeholder update

**Use when:** you have rough bullet-point status notes and need a clean written
update for execs, cross-functional partners, or a newsletter-style project update.

**Input:** your raw bullets — progress, blockers, decisions needed, whatever you'd
normally jot down before writing the "real" version.

---

```
Turn these raw notes into a stakeholder update. Requirements:

- Lead with the single most important thing the reader needs to know — status,
  a risk, or a decision needed — not a chronological recap of the week.
- Group into: Progress / Risks & blockers / Decisions needed from the reader /
  Next steps. Skip any section that's genuinely empty rather than padding it.
- Keep it skimmable: short paragraphs or bullets, no jargon the reader wouldn't
  already use themselves.
- If a "decision needed" exists, state the options and your recommendation —
  don't just surface the open question and leave the reader to do the framing.
- Match this tone: [e.g. "concise and direct," "matches our team's Friday
  update format," paste an example if you have one]
- Length target: [e.g. "fits in one screen without scrolling"]

Raw notes:

[PASTE YOUR BULLETS HERE]
```
